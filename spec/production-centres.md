# Production centres

Written by the main session at promotion, never by a research task. The summary describes only
`supported` and `corroborated` claims. Everything else stays in the table.

## Summary

*Isle Wars Pro*, unregistered, as read from its code: exactly three production centres exist, placed at every game start (and at every centre-move event) as three distinct countries drawn from a fresh 46-country permutation, with no spacing, adjacency or island constraint (C-CEN-01, C-CEN-03, C-CEN-04). A centre-increase event doubles the armies standing on all three, capped at 999, regardless of owner — and the doubled stacks never enter the income formula, which reads country holdings and complete islands only (C-CEN-02). The centre list survives ownership changes, and no human-path handler reads it; the computer's placement, dice and move handlers do (C-CEN-05).
