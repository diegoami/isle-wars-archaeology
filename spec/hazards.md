# Hazards

Written by the main session at promotion, never by a research task. The summary describes only
`supported` and `corroborated` claims. Everything else stays in the table.

## Summary

*Isle Wars Pro*, unregistered, as read from its code: five event kinds are drawn from a fourteen-slot table — earthquake and flood (one shared handler), rebellion, centre increase and centre move (C-HAZ-01) — at one shared between-turn dispatch that is not seat-blind: the concession and stop routes can return before the event branch and pre-empt an otherwise-eligible draw (C-HAZ-02). The draw happens before the overrides, giving conditional eligible-dispatch chances of 1-in-8, 14 or 21 by the frequency setting (Normal by default and locked in the unregistered build; Never overrides the drawn outcome), suppressed in the first six player-turns, while any seat holds more than 38 countries, at game over, and on the transition right after an event — the event frame's counter reset to 5 acts as a one-transition cooldown, for mean spacings of 9, 15 and 22 transitions under continuous eligibility (C-HAZ-03). The flood strikes a block of three or four consecutive countries — never country 1 — and replaces their armies with a uniform draw bounded by the *new* count, not the loss (C-HAZ-04, C-HAZ-05). The rebellion flips one country of an equal-lot eligible seat (two or more seats with three or more countries), changing no armies (C-HAZ-06). No event zeroes a positive stack, eliminates a seat, destroys a centre or targets a leader (C-HAZ-07); the centre increase helps whoever holds the centres, and the computer's centre read in its dice handler is a post-capture allocation input, not a risk threshold (C-HAZ-08).
