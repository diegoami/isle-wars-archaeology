# Reinforcement

Written by the main session at promotion, never by a research task. The summary describes only
`supported` and `corroborated` claims. Everything else stays in the table.

## Summary

*Isle Wars Pro*, unregistered, as read from its code: a seat earns armies once per turn, at turn start, immediately after the turn advance — its country count divided by 3 and rounded down, raised to 2 when below 2 (the floor touches the country part alone), plus the fixed bonus of every island it holds completely (C-REI-01, C-REI-02, C-REI-03). No production centre, difficulty or event term enters, and captures made during the turn never feed back into that turn's income. A card trade-in is the only later addition, after income and before placement: 5, 8 or 15 armies by kind, a doubling of the whole pool, or nothing for the bombing match; the human trades on an optional screen, at most once a turn, while the computer trades automatically, one set, by a fixed priority that takes 15 over doubling even when doubling would pay more (C-REI-03). The whole pool must be placed this turn: the human clicks any of its own countries in any split, with no way to end placement early or keep armies (a country's stored count clamps at 999, tested on the stack plus the allocation); the computer credits the whole pool inside its one turn-start handler, though its pool global is not reliably zeroed at return (C-REI-04, C-REI-05). Nothing carries over: the pool is zeroed and recomputed at every turn start.
