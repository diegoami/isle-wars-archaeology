# Combat

Written by the main session at promotion, never by a research task. The summary describes only
`supported` and `corroborated` claims. Everything else stays in the table.

## Summary

*Isle Wars Pro*, unregistered, as read from its code: each exchange is one die against one die, the attacker wins only on a strictly higher die, and the loser of the exchange loses one army (C-CMB-04 to C-CMB-06). A human may attack with at least 2 armies and at least as many as the target; the computer needs more than a drawn floor and more than the target (C-CMB-01 to C-CMB-03). A human attacker worn down to 1 army loses its own country; the computer's attacks never forfeit (C-CMB-09). The computer attacks in drawn rounds and decides whether to stop after each pass (C-CMB-08, C-CMB-10). Whether attacking pays overall is not answered (C-CMB-12).
