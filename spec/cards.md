# Cards

Written by the main session at promotion, never by a research task. The summary describes only
`supported` and `corroborated` claims. Everything else stays in the table.

## Summary

*Isle Wars Pro*, unregistered, as read from its code: cards come only from captures — one draw per capturing turn, human and computer alike, from a fixed 34-entry list that is never shuffled, depleted or refused (C-CRD-01 to C-CRD-03). A hand holds five; a draw into a full hand forces one discard first (the human clicks one away, the computer discards by a fixed priority), and drawing the bomb empties the whole hand (C-CRD-04). A trade-in is at most one three-of-a-kind set per turn — up to two wildcards count in detection — at the top of the turn, adding 5, 8 or 15 armies, doubling the pool, or bombing without adding; the computer trades automatically by a fixed priority and never skips, and its removal step is not wildcard-capped (C-CRD-05). The bomb replaces the victim's armies with a uniform draw of 1 to half, changing no ownership; the computer's target sampling indexes the army table with a sampled army count, so its selection rule is unestablished (C-CRD-06). An anti-bomb cancels one bomb against its owner and is consumed; the human's protected branch reorders the bomber's own hand through stale picks and can clear retained cards there (C-CRD-07).
