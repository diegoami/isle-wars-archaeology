## Answer

- **State is seen on the screen; three files carry parts of it.** The owner and army count of each of the 46 countries, whose turn it is, the phase, the cards counts per colour, the result of an attack and the end of a game are all on the screen (screenshots of E005-r0011, E005-r0012 and E005-r0037: one human turn, an attack, the computer turns). Three files are written, all comma-separated decimal text with CRLF line ends: `ISLEWAR.SCR` by the first `files` step of every run (38 of 38 here, 12 of 12 in F005), `ISLEWAR.DFT` after Set level or Default, and `ISLEWAR.SAV` after Save. `ISLEWAR.DAT` (shipped) was never written in any run.
- **`ISLEWAR.DFT` holds the level and the starting armies chosen.** After Set level it is one line ` L , A , 2 ` (spaces around the commas and at both ends, CRLF). The first field moved with the level (Easy 1, Normal 2) and the second with the starting armies (1, 3) in runs that held the other choice (rows D1 to D3). Hard wrote 3 and Hardest 4 in the first field, and 6 and 10 starting armies wrote 6 and 10 in the second, but only in runs that changed both choices at once (E005-r0009; E005-r0010 and E005-r0038): that these values belong to the level and the armies rests on the fields' roles shown at Easy and Normal, not on a pair of runs at Hard or Hardest. The third field was 2 in every run and is not decoded (D4). Default writes three lines `1`, `3`, `2` (9 bytes, E005-r0001, E005-r0002, E005-r0027, E005-r0028). After a conceded game, Another game and Load saved game the file read three lines ` 1 `, ` 1 `, ` 2 ` (E005-r0018, E005-r0019).
- **`ISLEWAR.SCR` counts conceded games at Easy and at Normal.** Four lines of two numbers, `0,0` four times at launch (20 bytes). Quit and Yes (conceding) at Easy made line 1 `0, 1` (E005-r0014, E005-r0015, E005-r0031, E005-r0032; also the exit arm, E005-r0021, E005-r0036); at Normal line 2 (E005-r0023, E005-r0024). No record captured the scoreboard after a concession (the correction below). The first number of each line, and lines 3 and 4, never changed: that the first number is the wins and that lines 3 and 4 are Hard and Hardest is not shown (D7, D8). The changed line was not yet in the file at the `files` step taken after the first change of the screen that followed Yes (step 19 of the concede arms: `0,0` in all six concede runs, the screen showing `GAME OVER` over the board with the `CONCEDE GAME ?` panel still drawn) and was there at the next `files` step, after one more click (at 480,236) and the `GAME OVER` picture with `ANOTHER GAME ?` (step 22; 0.66 to 0.67 s after that click in E005-r0031, E005-r0032). Which screen was up when the game wrote the file, and what the click at 480,236 landed on, no record shows.

## Proposed representation rows

These are proposals for `spec/representation.md`; IDs are assigned at promotion. Scope `DOS`, set `iw-dos-3.1-30121376`. ## Method

**Artifact.** Set `iw-dos-3.1-30121376`. `setup/iw-dos/iw-dos-runtime.sh` printed `ready` before the runs and again after them; `IW_DOS_DIR` was read and hashed only. Every run is a T11 cycle with its seal unchanged: the run copy checked with `check --strict` before the launch and immediately before it, only the run copy mounted as C:, `IW_DOS_DIR` bound read-only, the whole run in a network namespace of its own (the one interface DOSBox-X sees is `lo`), `completed` only on a clean finish.

**Runtime.** `runtime/iw-dos/spike/cycle.py --experiment E005 --steps <script>` at the commit named in each record, DOSBox-X 2024.03.01, Xvfb 800x600x24, no window manager. T14's additions: `--experiment` (records and bundles under E005), `--steps` (a step script in place of the one click on Set level: `navsteps.py`, parsed and refused before anything starts), `board.py` (reading owners and borders from a screenshot, to find a Blue country that touches an enemy on a board that differs between runs), and a record of every file the game adds or changes (size and sha256; the bytes, in hex when at most 64 and as a copy in the bundle, only for a new `ISLEWAR.SCR`, `ISLEWAR.DFT` or `ISLEWAR.SAV` that is no file of the registered set). Each script's sha256 is an observation of its record. A script can only click the game's window, press the keys `Return`, `space`, `Escape`, `c` and `e`, and wait; a line outside that grammar, or a click on the shareware notices' buttons, is refused before the launch (`tests/test_iw_dos_steps.py`; 57 mutants of the seal and of these additions are killed by `tests/mutants_iw_dos.py`).

**Round 1 (PR #58).** The records from E005-r0027 were made with the cycle as corrected after the round-0 review: every wait is required (a time-out aborts the run); a click waits for a still screen and is refused on the notices' buttons and while the notices are up; no probe runs with a step script; every record declares its screenshots (I1); clicks, step ends, the window and each capture are timed from the launch. The round-0 records E005-r0001 to E005-r0026 were made with waits whose time-out did not abort. Their observations show no required wait that timed out in a completed record (`measurements/round1/analysis-20261005T113945Z/waits.tsv`; the one time-out in a completed record, E005-r0033's first click on Save, was followed by a second click that changed the screen, as `click-change` does); the stillness before a click was not recorded before E005-r0027.

## Inferences

- From D2 and D3: the DFT's first two fields are the level and the starting armies chosen, because at Easy and Normal, and at 1 and 3 armies, each moves with its choice and not with the other.
- From D8: `ISLEWAR.SCR` keeps a figure per level that goes up when a game is conceded; nothing here says what a won game writes.
- From D10: the owner codes 9, 4, 2, 8 match the EGA colour indexes of light blue, red, green and dark grey (the Brown army is drawn dark grey). That is a remark on the numbers; only the mapping is tested.

## Alternatives considered

- **The owner mapping is circular: the screen reader and the codes were fitted to each other.** Not excluded as a fit, excluded as a coincidence. The codes were read off the first save and then tested unchanged on all 12 saves: 552 readings, none against. The reader's totals equal the four figures of the game's own scoreboard panel, read by eye, on 7 of 8 screenshots (`board-owner-check.tsv`); the 8th (E005-r0011 `attack-result.png`) shows the conquered country already Blue on the map while the panel still reads 17, and the panel reads 18 after the move in. The mapping from code to colour is one-to-one (`save-codes.tsv`).
- **The save's armies are read from the screen, not the file.** The armies of the countries that changed are checked against the digits on the screenshot by eye for E005-r0011 (countries 3, 7, 25 and 33: 3, 3, 4, 4), the conquered country in E005-r0012, and E005-r0037 (countries 5, 9, 13, 17, 32, 34, 42: 2, 3, 2, 3, 2, 3, 2); the other countries were not compared one by one (a digit reader is not in this task).
- **A change in the second save is caused by the computer, not by the action.** For the conquered country the action explains the change in all three runs (owner to 9, armies 1 to the number moved in); the other differences are in countries the computer's turns touched and are not claimed.
- **The runs agree because the reset hid what run 1 changed.** Excluded as in F005: every run starts from the same run copy (checked by `start_state.sha256`, every record) and every diff is the files the game added.
- **The savestate is the original's behaviour.** It is I2 and says so; nothing from it is claimed about the game.
- **A round-0 record was completed past a wait that had timed out** (PR #58 R7). Looked for in every record (`waits.tsv`): none.

## What this does not establish

- *Isle Wars Pro*: nothing; this finding is DOS *Isle Wars*' only (rule 19).
- Any game rule: the numbers above are what the files held and what the screen showed in these runs, not how the game plays. In the three attacks the ATTACK button needed two, two and three clicks before the move-in panel showed, and the attacker's count shown in the message was one more than the armies moved in; neither is claimed as a rule.
- A win, a hazard, a card trade-in, MOVE and CANCEL, a level above Easy played past the first turn, a save at Hard or Hardest in a record, more than one save in a run at one turn, and what the registered version does (unknown).
- The meaning of the DFT's third field, of the save's two constant lines and last line, of SCR's first numbers and of its lines 3 and 4; which choice sets the DFT's Hard and Hardest values; Red's place in the card counts; that the save's country list is the production centres (D12), that its two DFT values are the level and the armies one by one (D13), and that the card line's last three numbers are the other colours' cards (D14).
- The scoreboard after a concession: no record captured it (the round-2 correction under "Answer"), and which screen was up when the game wrote `ISLEWAR.SCR`.
- Which action rewrites the DFT in the line layout (D6), and whether a Save after placing armies writes anything (one exploration run, x26, says no).
- What Load restores beyond the owners (D16).
- Whether a Help screen exists (none reached; a Help key was not tried).
- Why start pictures repeat, or whether a start is drawn afresh.
- What `ISLEWAR.DAT` holds.
- The memory image: no game field was looked for (W5b).
- Repeatability beyond one machine and one DOSBox-X build; the arms that ran once are probes.
- Audio, real DOS hardware, another emulator.
