# F009 DOS Isle Wars' ISLEWAR.SAV is seven groups of text lines (the production centres, the level and the starting armies, Speed and Sound, 46 owners, 46 armies, four card counts, four hands), each shown by a pair of runs that differ in one action or by a crafted save the game loaded and showed; of the card digits, natural runs name only 3 (Wild) and 7 (5 Bonus), and the names of the other seven are I3-only candidates; a writer encodes a decoded position into the same bytes, and the game loaded each crafted save and wrote it back byte for byte

- **Status**: proposed
- **Question**: What does each line of DOS *Isle Wars*' `ISLEWAR.SAV` mean, as shown by pairs of runs that differ in one stated action (F007 left the production-centre list, the two DFT values, the other colours' cards, the two constant lines and the last line as candidates or unknown, and R010 and R011 confounded the level with the starting armies); does a writer that encodes a decoded position give the game's own bytes for every natural save; and does the game load a save the writer wrote and show the written values (E007, task T16, W4b follow-on)?
- **Tests claims**: R010, R011, R017, R018, R019, R020, R021, R022, R023, R024, C-SET-05, C-SET-06 (the rows of F007 this finding settles or narrows; it makes no claim about a game rule). Each field below names the rows it bears on.
- **Experiment and runs**: E007; runs E007-r0001 … E007-r0046, all in `runs/E007/`. 43 completed and 3 aborted (E007-r0013, E007-r0015, E007-r0024, all the arm `attack-pass`: a click that changed nothing at turn 3, and two attacks that never reached the move-in panel); the aborted records stay, and their saves are in the tables as natural saves. E007-r0028 … E007-r0046 are the 19 runs that load a crafted save (arms `load-*`); the others are natural. **E007-r0001 to E007-r0005 are cleaned copies, not raw records**: before their first commit, one string of each record's `actions.args` (the `--steps` path) was replaced by the repository-relative path (`runs/E007/CORRECTIONS.md`; declared, not re-run, by the owner's decision of 2026-10-05). Every other part of those records, their manifests and their bundles is as the cycle wrote it. They are marked "(cleaned)" wherever this finding cites them, and no claim rests on them alone: each field they show is also shown by a pair without them (the pairs L2, L4, L6, A2, A3b, A4b, A5b, S2, S4, M2 and C1 below).
- **Artifact**: set `iw-dos-3.1-30121376`, variant none. No run changed a file of the set: every file the game wrote, and every crafted save, is a new file of the run copy, removed by the reset (every record: "run copy after reset equals the run copy before launch: yes"; `setup/iw-dos/iw-dos-runtime.sh` printed `ready` and `register_artifact.py check --strict` exited 0 after the runs: `experiments/E007-iw-dos-save-format/measurements/done-when/`).
- **Max intervention**: I3. The 19 `load-*` runs place a crafted save in the run copy (`vocabulary/interventions.md`: "a crafted or modified save" is I3); each declares it in its record with the sha256 of the position it was written from and of the save. Every other run is I1 (screenshots) and reads the game's own files (I0). Each I3 result is corroborated at I0 below, or says that it is not (the card names of digits other than 3 and 7 are not).
- **Tier reached**: V2 (the original, headless, under DOSBox-X; no real DOS hardware or other emulator, so no V3)
- **Confidence**: supported for every line of the layout, and for every field meaning but one, at the shareware 3.1 build under DOSBox-X on one machine, in one day. The level, the starting armies, Speed, Sound and the card counts each moved in line with a stated action in two or more runs and, for Speed, Sound and the counts, also in a crafted save that the game showed. The owners, armies and centres were shown by a crafted save and by natural saves read against the screen (66 natural saves against their screenshots, none differing). The exception is the names of the card digits: that the last line holds one digit per card, in the order Blue, Green, Red, Brown, is supported, and natural draws name two digits (3 Wild, 7 5 Bonus); the names of 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 come only from the pictures of two crafted hands (I3), so they are candidates, not decoded meanings from the original (1 and 2 occur in natural saves, but no natural run showed their card). Values outside what was tried (armies above 99, a hand above 5, a level or a starting-armies value other than the four offered, the order of the centres) are not claimed. A save that the game writes after loading a crafted one is the game's own output of what it loaded, not a natural save, and is kept apart in every table.

## Answer

### The layout, each line shown by runs

- **A save is text with CRLF line ends and nothing else** (R017). Every one of the 50 distinct natural saves of E005 and E007 and the 17 distinct saves of the crafted runs decodes into the seven groups below and encodes back to the same bytes: E007-r0001 (cleaned), E007-r0003 (cleaned), E007-r0012, E007-r0014, E007-r0025, E005-E007-r0011, E005-E007-r0037, and the others in `saves/roundtrip.tsv`.
- **Line 1 is the number N of production centres, and the next N lines are their country numbers** (R020). The list equals the stars on the screen (the manual's "(*) below the City number") in all 66 natural saves compared with their screenshot, and the stars follow a list that was written: the control E007-r0028 shows stars at 4, 11 and 29, and the crafted list 1, 2, 3 shows them at 1, 2 and 3 and at no other country (E007-r0031). The list is that of the board, not of the turn or the level: it is the same in all the saves of one game, 4 saves and 7 saves (E007-r0012, E007-r0014), after a conquest and a card drawn (E007-r0025, E007-r0026, E007-r0027), and differs between two games started with the same choices (pair C1: E007-r0026, E007-r0027; also E007-r0001 (cleaned) against E007-r0023). What moves a centre inside a game was not found: no action tried did.
- **The next two lines are the level and the starting armies of the game** (R021, R010, R011, C-SET-05). The first is 1 at Easy, 2 at Normal, 3 at Hard and 4 at Hardest, each value in two pairs of runs that change the level alone at 1 starting army, one of them without a cleaned record: Normal L1 (E007-r0001 (cleaned), E007-r0002 (cleaned)) and L2 (E007-r0023, E007-r0021); Hard L3 (E007-r0001 (cleaned), E007-r0003 (cleaned)) and L4 (E007-r0023, E007-r0019); Hardest L5 (E007-r0001 (cleaned), E007-r0004 (cleaned)) and L6 (E007-r0023, E007-r0020). This settles R010 and R011 for the level. The second is 1, 3, 6 or 10, the starting armies, in the pairs that change the armies alone: 3 at Easy in A1 (E007-r0001 (cleaned), E007-r0005 (cleaned)) and A2 (E007-r0023, E007-r0022); 6 and 10 at Easy against the control E007-r0023 (A3b with E007-r0006, A4b with E007-r0007) and against E007-r0001 (cleaned) (A3, A4); 6 at Hard against E007-r0019 (A5b) and E007-r0003 (cleaned) (A5), all with E007-r0008. The 6 and 10 arms ran once each (E007-r0006, E007-r0007, E007-r0008), so those values rest on one treatment run each against two controls: probes, not replicated. A crafted pair 3 and 6 was loaded and the game wrote it into `ISLEWAR.DFT` as ` 3 `, ` 6 `, ` 2 ` (E007-r0034, I3; the same file in the control E007-r0028 reads ` 1 `, ` 1 `, ` 2 `), which says that Load saved game takes the level and the starting armies from these two lines.
- **The next two lines are Speed and Sound** (R023, the two lines F007 called constant). The first is the Speed value (1 to 9; 2 at launch): Faster once gives 1 (E007-r0009, E007-r0017), Slower once gives 3 (E007-r0010, E007-r0018), and a crafted 5 makes the Speed panel read 5 (E007-r0037, I3). The second is Sound, 0 on and 1 off: one click on Sound gives 1 (E007-r0011, E007-r0016), and a crafted 1 makes the next click on Sound show `ON...` where the control's shows `OFF..` (E007-r0046 against E007-r0045, I3). Each has a pair against E007-r0023 (S2, S4, M2), so none rests on the cleaned control E007-r0001. The third value of `ISLEWAR.DFT` (F007 D4, D5, D6) is the Speed value: ` 1 `, ` 1 `, ` 1 ` after Faster and Save (E007-r0009, E007-r0017), ` 1 `, ` 1 `, ` 3 ` after Slower and Save (E007-r0010, E007-r0018), ` 1 `, ` 1 `, ` 5 ` after Load of the crafted 5 (E007-r0037).
- **The next 46 lines are the owners of countries 1 to 46, as codes 9 Blue, 4 Red, 2 Green and 8 Brown** (R018, R024). A crafted owner shows on the screen: country 2 from Red to Blue (E007-r0029: Blue 18 countries, Red 10, the prompt `PLACE 6 ARMIES`), and country 12 from Blue to Red, Green and Brown (E007-r0039, E007-r0040, E007-r0041); the owners read from the pixels equal the crafted owners in 46 of 46 countries in each of the 19 crafted runs, and every other field the screen shows equals the control's (the table in Observations). That code 9 is Blue was shown in F007 by a conquest (R018), and Load brought the natural owners back (R024).
- **The next 46 lines are the army counts of countries 1 to 46** (R019, R024). A crafted count shows: 7 (E007-r0030), 99 (E007-r0032) and 0 (E007-r0033) on country 12, and nothing else of the board differs from the control's but the pointer arrow and the prompt text.
- **The next line, `a , b , c , d`, is the number of cards held by Blue, Red, Green and Brown** (R022). The scoreboard's `C>` figure of each colour equals it in all 68 natural saves that have a screenshot of the turn before the Save (the map from the figure's pixels to the count is one to one: 0, 1, 2 and 3), and a card count written for one colour shows in that colour's row and no other: Blue (E007-r0035), Red (E007-r0042), Green (E007-r0043), Brown (E007-r0044). Each count moves with the computers' turns: a colour's count goes up by one, and its hand grows by one digit, when it draws (E007-r0012, E007-r0014), and Blue's goes from 0 to 1 when Blue conquers a country and passes (E007-r0025, E007-r0026, E007-r0027) and stays 0 when Blue passes without attacking (E007-r0012, E007-r0014). Natural counts reached 3; a count of 5 was seen only in crafted hands (E007-r0036, E007-r0038, I3).
- **The last line is the cards held, one digit per card, in the order Blue, Green, Red, Brown** (R023, R022). The field of each colour is as long as that colour's count in all 50 natural saves, and no other order of the four fields fits them all (the best other order fits 39 of 50); a card count crafted for one colour lands in that colour's field (E007-r0035, E007-r0042, E007-r0043, E007-r0044). A new card is added at the end of the hand (E007-r0012, E007-r0014). Natural saves held only the digits 1, 2, 3 and 7 (`hand-order/card-digits-seen.tsv`: E007-r0012, E007-r0014).
- **Two digits are named by natural runs; the other seven names are I3-only candidates** (R023). Natural draws of Blue's show the card on the CARD screen: a 7 shows a `5 BONUS` card and a 3 a `WILD` card (E007-r0025, E007-r0026, E007-r0027; I0 with I1 screenshots), and the crafted hands agree for both. These are what the game drew for those digits when they were loaded, not a meaning shown from the original's own play: 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 never occurred in a natural save, and 1 and 2 occurred only in the computers' hands, whose cards the screen does not show. They stay candidates until a natural run shows them, or T26's static reading of the executable settles the table (open).
- **The game accepted every crafted save and wrote it back unchanged** (R024, C-SET-06). In 17 of the 17 crafted runs that did not change a setting after the load, the game's own second Save equals the crafted save byte for byte (E007-r0028, E007-r0029, E007-r0030, E007-r0031, E007-r0032, E007-r0033, E007-r0034, E007-r0035, E007-r0036, E007-r0037, E007-r0038, E007-r0039, E007-r0040, E007-r0041, E007-r0042, E007-r0043, E007-r0044); in the two runs that clicked Sound before the second Save, the second Save equals the crafted save but for the Sound line, as the click says (E007-r0045, E007-r0046). No crafted value was refused or changed by the game.

### The writer

- **The writer writes a position as the game's own bytes** (R017). `runtime/iw-dos/save/savefmt.py` decodes the bytes of a save into a position and encodes a position back, and encode followed by decode returns the position; `encode(decode(bytes))` equals the bytes for each of the 50 distinct natural saves of E005 and E007 (E007-r0001 (cleaned), E007-r0003 (cleaned), E007-r0012, E007-r0014, E007-r0025, E005-E007-r0011, E005-E007-r0037, and the others in `saves/roundtrip.tsv`). It refuses a position outside the values that were seen or tried (the domain is in the module's constants, each with the runs it comes from: static:runtime/iw-dos/save/savefmt.py:35), as in E007-r0032 and E007-r0036. An edit names a country by a whole number from 1 to 46 and a hand by a colour; anything else is refused (`writer.edit`, since round 1).
- **The writer places a save only in a run copy, and only as `ISLEWAR.SAV`.** `runtime/iw-dos/save/writer.py` writes into `<run directory>/game` of a run directory outside every checkout that is not `IW_DOS_DIR` under any name and holds the registered set intact, creating the file new; the cycle's `put-save` step calls it after the launch checks and before Load saved game, and the reset removes the file (E007-r0028, E007-r0029).

## Method

**Artifact.** Set `iw-dos-3.1-30121376`. `setup/iw-dos/iw-dos-runtime.sh` printed `ready` before the runs and again after them; `IW_DOS_DIR` was read and hashed only. Every run is a T11 cycle with its seal unchanged: the run copy checked with `check --strict` before the launch and immediately before it, only the run copy mounted as C:, `IW_DOS_DIR` bound read-only, the whole run in a network namespace of its own, `completed` only on a clean finish. The run directory and the bundle folder were this task's own (another task ran DOSBox-X on the machine; the cycle refuses to start while another run's process is alive, so `scripts/run-arms.sh` waited and tried again, and touched no process but its own).

**Runtime.** `runtime/iw-dos/spike/cycle.py --experiment E007 --steps <script>` at the commit named in each record (E007-r0001 (cleaned) to E007-r0005 (cleaned) at 8edce92, E007-r0006 to E007-r0014 at 7a6faae, E007-r0015 to E007-r0038 at 05712d3, E007-r0039 to E007-r0044 at f815bcd, E007-r0045 and E007-r0046 at a65c1eb; the commits after 7a6faae changed the cycle not at all, and f815bcd narrowed the writer's domain), DOSBox-X 2024.03.01, Xvfb 800x600x24, no window manager. T16's additions to the cycle: `--experiment E007`, the step `put-save` and the argument `--crafted` (the position the step writes; refused when the script has one and the run the other, when the position cannot be encoded, and when the run copy is not a run copy), and the I3 declaration in the record. Each script's sha256 is an observation of its record.

**The five cleaned records (review R1).** The first batch, E007-r0001 to E007-r0005, was started with the step script given by an absolute path of the machine, which the cycle records in `actions.args`. Before any of them was committed, that one string in each record was replaced by the repository-relative path; nothing else was changed (`runs/E007/CORRECTIONS.md`). So their `actions.args` is not raw, while their observations, the script's sha256 among them, and their manifests and bundles are. The owner decided on 2026-10-05 to declare this and not to re-run them. To keep every claim off these five alone, each field they support also has a pair without them: level L2, L4, L6; starting armies A2, A3b, A4b, A5b (the last three built in round 1 from the existing records, `measurements/round1-pairs-20261005T152813Z/`, with `pairs-round1.json`); the centres of two games with the same choices, C1; Speed and Sound S2, S4, M2; the round-trip reads the saves in the bundles, which the cleaning did not touch, and also covers the natural saves of E005 and of E007-r0006 to E007-r0027. The crafted saves were made from the first save of E007-r0001 (cleaned); that save is a bundle file recorded by its sha256 in the manifest, which the cleaning did not touch, and the game loaded it and wrote it back byte for byte in E007-r0028, a raw record.

## Inferences

- **The save is the position a Load restores, and the writer is complete for what was shown** (R024, C-SET-06). The game reproduced the position byte for byte from a position the writer encoded in 17 runs, one field changed each, so each field is read and written back by the game as the decoder says: E007-r0028, E007-r0029, E007-r0030, E007-r0031, E007-r0034, E007-r0035, E007-r0037, E007-r0039, E007-r0042. That the game keeps a digit it was given says nothing about what the digit's card is.
- **The two lines F007 called constant are Speed and Sound** (R023). They moved with a click on the button in two runs each and with a crafted value in one: E007-r0009, E007-r0017, E007-r0010, E007-r0018, E007-r0011, E007-r0016, E007-r0037, E007-r0046.
- **The DFT pair of the save is the level and the starting armies of the game, one by one** (R021, R010, R011, C-SET-05). Each moved with its own choice in two or more pairs, at least one of them without a cleaned record, and the game wrote the crafted pair into `ISLEWAR.DFT` at Load, which shows that the save and the file are one setting: E007-r0021, E007-r0019, E007-r0020, E007-r0022, E007-r0006, E007-r0007, E007-r0008, E007-r0034 (and E007-r0002 (cleaned) to E007-r0005 (cleaned)).
- **The list of centres is the board's stars** (R020). It matched the screen in every natural save, was drawn where it was written, and did not change inside a game under any action tried: E007-r0031, E007-r0012, E007-r0014, E007-r0025.
- **The last line is the hands, one digit per card; 3 is Wild and 7 is 5 Bonus** (R023, R022). The order of its fields is fixed by the lengths in every natural save and by the crafted counts of each colour; the two digits that natural draws named on Blue's CARD screen agree with the pictures of the crafted hands: E007-r0025, E007-r0026, E007-r0036, E007-r0038, E007-r0042, E007-r0043, E007-r0044. The names of 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 are candidates from crafted hands only (I3), with no I0 corroboration.
- **Positions can be set for an experiment.** An experiment that starts from a chosen position can write it and load it (owners, armies, the cards of each colour, the settings), and the game plays on from it; what the game does from such a position was not tested: E007-r0029, E007-r0030, E007-r0035, E007-r0042. A position that holds a card other than 3 or 7 is set by digit, and which card the game takes it for is a candidate (I3).

## Alternatives considered

- **A line follows the board, not the action.** For the settings, the level and the starting armies the pairs differ in one action and the field changes by the same step in every pair, while the board's fields differ at random between runs: excluded for these by the six level pairs, the eight armies pairs (A1 to A5, A3b, A4b, A5b), the four Speed pairs and the two Sound pairs; and for Speed and Sound again by a crafted value that the screen showed (E007-r0037, E007-r0046). It is the answer for the centres, which follow the board.
- **A field rests on the five cleaned records only.** Excluded: every field E007-r0001 (cleaned) to E007-r0005 (cleaned) show is also shown by a pair of raw records (L2, L4, L6, A2, A3b, A4b, A5b, C1, S2, S4, M2), and their cleaning changed only one string of `actions.args`, not an observation, a manifest or a bundle file (`runs/E007/CORRECTIONS.md`).
- **The crafted saves are shown by the game only because the writer and the decoder share a mistake.** Excluded for the owners, armies, centres, counts and Speed by the screen, which does not use the decoder (the board reader, the star matcher and the `C>` patterns are separate code), and for every field by the game's own second Save, which equals the crafted bytes: E007-r0029, E007-r0030, E007-r0031, E007-r0035, E007-r0037. Not excluded for the Sound and the level lines as the screen shows them: Sound is shown by the label after one click (E007-r0046), the level by the `ISLEWAR.DFT` the game writes (E007-r0034), not by the play.
- **The card digits are an order of the game's list and not its names, or a crafted digit is drawn as a card it does not stand for in play.** Not excluded for any digit but 3 and 7: the names of the other seven rest on the pictures of ten crafted cards (E007-r0036, E007-r0038, I3) and on no natural run; the icons of 8 and 9 have no text, and their names (a bomb, a bomb crossed out; the manual's Bomb and Anti-Bomb) are by the pictures. A natural run that shows Blue drawing those cards, or T26's static reading of the card table, would settle it.
- **A run was completed past a wait that had timed out.** Excluded: every wait is required in this cycle and a time-out aborts the run, as in E005 (the three aborted records are what a failed wait looks like): E007-r0013, E007-r0015, E007-r0024.
- **The second Save equals the crafted save because the game never re-wrote it.** Excluded: the Sound line of the second Save differs from the crafted one in the two runs that clicked Sound first, so the game wrote that file, and `ISLEWAR.DFT` is written at the Load with the loaded pair and speed: E007-r0045, E007-r0046, E007-r0034, E007-r0037.

## What this does not establish

- *Isle Wars Pro*: nothing; this finding is DOS *Isle Wars*' only (rule 19). The registered build is not measured either.
- Any game rule: what a production centre does in play (the manual says the armies on it double at a "Production Center increase", an event no run saw), what the level changes in the computers' play, what the card digits do when played, and what the game does from a crafted position beyond showing it.
- The names of the card digits 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 in the original's own play: they are I3-only candidates (open; T26's static triage may settle them from the code, or a natural run in which Blue draws them). 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 were never seen in a natural save.
- What moves a production centre inside a game: nothing tried did (pass-only turns, a conquest and a card drawn); a hazard, the loss of a centre's country or another action may.
- The order of the centres in the list (it is not sorted in natural saves, for example 29, 4, 11, and was sorted in the crafted one), and whether the order matters to the game.
- Values the writer refuses: more than 99 armies, a hand of more than 5 cards, a level or starting armies other than the four offered, a Speed outside 1 to 9, more than 5 centres. The game's own limits were not looked for. A natural count above 3 was not seen.
- Which of the Speed click and the Save rewrote `ISLEWAR.DFT` (the file was one line before and three lines after both: E007-r0009).
- Whether a save is accepted at a turn other than the start of a human turn: the saves are all of that kind.
- Repeatability beyond one machine and one DOSBox-X build; the arms that ran once (Easy 6, Easy 10, Hard 6) are probes, each against two controls. The boards repeat across runs (the same centres in several games), and nothing here says why.
- That the five cleaned records' `actions.args` is what the cycle wrote: it is not (`runs/E007/CORRECTIONS.md`).
- Audio, real DOS hardware, another emulator.
