- **Hazards**:
  - **A misread screen**: T13's pixels-by-region path; a region outside the seal's viewport
    loses its reference. Stay inside the regions E004's manifest defines.
  - **Hard-coding a count target** in the recorder (rule 22 hazard): the finding's T28 plan
    names the count, not the recorder; the recorder reads the plan, the main session runs
    the count, the outcome is the count that the run produced.
  - **Over-claiming "corroborated"** when play only agrees in one or two cases (rule 22):
    narrow to `narrowed` with the per-arm count, and write the discrepancy in the outcome's
    "what would change it". The 80–90% rule applies; a 100% match is over-claimed.
  - **A run of the wrong build** (rule 19): T13's seal is the gate; the cycle only records
    `completed` when the prefix matches the inventory. A failed seal is `aborted` and the
    record is discarded.
  - **The deal is random** (F018): a "the deal does X" claim is corroborated over at least
    10 deals, never one; the per-finding batch's plan names the count.
  - **The fast-forward flag (F016, F019)**: after the human's elimination, the prompt
    changes the play-out; T28 records both branches (accept the prompt vs. refuse it) and
    names the branch the outcome belongs to.
  - **The screen-walk task (T24)** is not a follow-up: the rules T28 corroborates are the
    Lingo ones, and T24 walks screens for layout and timing, not rules.
  - **The C3 lines are not the implementer's**: the main session runs them, posts the records
    on the PR, and the implementer turns the records into outcomes (L26).
