  of malpaco's economy, and Q-BON-05's capture-draw rule decides how much attacking pays.

- **Scope**:
  - **Protects:** rule 18 (no line of decompiled code in the repository, the PR or a comment; describe
    in our own words, cite script number, name and handler); a static reading is a reading (every claim
    says it is read from code, with a confidence, and none is stated as observed behaviour of the
    running game); rule 19 (Pro only, set `iwp-2.0-996e86c5`, unregistered).
  - **F014** (proposed), template headings as F011's to F013's, for each of the 7 questions:
    - the answer as read, in our words, with its numbers: when a card is given (the capture draw and
      its gate, the computer's draw on its captures), the kinds and how the list or draw weights them,
      what makes a match (including wildcards and their cap), what each kind's trade-in adds (these
      must agree with F013's 5, 8, 15, doubling and bomb, or say where they do not), the hand limit
      and how it is enforced at a further draw, the bomb's effect on its victim and the anti-bomb
      protection, and how often a trade-in can happen;

    - its confidence (rule 22), and what would change it;
    - its corroboration plan for T28 (what to count in recorded play), or `static-only` with why;
    - where the human's and the computer's paths differ, both.
  - Tables a later task can use, in our own words: the card-kind table (kind, how the list or draw
    produces it, what its trade-in adds) and the draw rules.
