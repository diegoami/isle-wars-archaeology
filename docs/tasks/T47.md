- **Scope**:
  - **The player extends** T45's human-seat player with the move phase and these cases. Per
    session, on a turn in which the case picker says *move*:
    1. **Stacks 2, 3, 5 (Q-MOV-02).** Move **all**, **all-but-one**, **all-but-two** in one turn;
       record whether the move screen's counter can be taken to 0 (expected: no). Two trials per
       (stack, amount).
    2. **Stacks 2, 3, 5 (Q-OWN-01, human side).** From a stack of *N*, capture a neighbour of
       *N − 1*, choose the "all" move-in, record the source's `−n` and destination's `+n`. Three
       trials per stack.
    3. **Move-then-more (Q-MOV-01, Q-MOV-03).** In at least 10 turns with a move: try a *second*
       move (must fail); try an attack after a move (must fail); try a destination two borders away
       (must fail); capture a country and attack from it in the same turn (must succeed when the
       attack check passes).
  - **The records**: run/1 records under `runs/E021/session-a/`, exported as T45's, with
    `runtime.platform` `wine-on-user-machine-iwp-2.0` and `start_state.sha256` the F019
    `Islepro.exe` hash, so constraint F's gate accepts them. Evidence class `recording`. No
    original text in `observations[]` (constraint D).
  - **A progress number** (`measurements/progress-r<nn>.json`) is appended — never overwritten
    (rule 20) — next to T45's. It counts the per-question trials met across T47's sitting series.
  - **Not in scope:** computer-vs-computer exchanges (T44, T46); per-claim outcomes in a finding
    (T28's F021); move counts over many games as a result (Q-TRN-05, sits in the *Measured
    questions* half of T28 — out of scope for T47); quality of play (T28 carve-out E); computer
    captures (T45 plus a separate computer-seat player, later).
- **Done when**:

- **Hazards**:
  - **The "all" button caps at `source − 1`.** E020's screens' move counter stops at 1 (F011,
    Q-MOV-02). A move that tries to take the counter to 0 is the result, not a bug.
  - **Move-then-attack closes the turn** (F011, Q-MOV-01, rule 18): the frame after the move's
    confirm has only `Pass` and the turn-end buttons, not `Attack`. A reading that says otherwise
    is wrong; the post-move attack test catches it.
  - **Captures' armies can attack on the same turn** (F011, Q-MOV-03). The next-turn check is the
     `−1` source counter on the destination; if absent, refuse the attack observation.
  - **Original text** (constraint D): button labels and messages are named by their role, never
     quoted in records.
  - **The deal is random** (F018): a row may not appear in a single sitting; record what was looked
     for and not found; never force a board.
  - **New Game mid-game credits a loss** and moves the install to the successor set (F003, F019,
    #152). Reuse T45's title-only rule; never write your own New Game path.
  - **The implementer cannot run the game** (L26): it writes the move-phase extension and its
     tests; the main session runs the sittings and posts their output.
