  stacks capture only about 11–20% of the time; what a player earns per turn, and what an island is
  worth, decides whether taking and holding countries is worth that cost.

- **Scope**:
  - **Protects:** rule 18 (no line of decompiled code in the repository, the PR or a comment; describe
    in our own words, cite script number, name and handler); a static reading is a reading (every claim
    says it is read from code, with a confidence, and none is stated as observed behaviour of the
    running game); rule 19 (Pro only, set `iwp-2.0-996e86c5`, unregistered).
  - **F013** (proposed), template headings as F011's and F012's, for each of the 7 questions:
    - the answer as read, in our words, with its numbers: armies per turn and how they are computed
      (per country, a divisor, a minimum), each island's bonus and which countries make each island,
      when income is computed and when it is placed, and what the trade-in adds;
    - its sources: script number and name, handler, and the binary's sha256 (`static:decompile`);
    - its confidence (rule 22), and what would change it;
    - its corroboration plan for T28 (what to count in recorded play), or `static-only` with why;
    - where the human's and the computer's paths differ, both.
  - Tables a later task can use, in our own words: the island table (island, its countries by number,
    its bonus) and the income formula.

  - **Not in scope:** combat (F011), turn order and victory (F012), the cards themselves (T22: here only
    what a trade-in adds to income), centres and hazards (T23), the AI's choices (T27); any run.
