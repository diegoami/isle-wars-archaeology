    - T11's seal on every launch.

  - **Decode, from natural saves (I0):** for each of R020, R021 and R022, and for the confounded

    - the level alone at fixed armies;

    What no action can move stays unknown, with what was tried. The two constant lines and the last
    line (R023) are decoded if an action moves them; otherwise they stay unknown.

    - Load it.
    - Show on the screen that the game shows the written value, and that every other field
      observed equals the natural save's.

    Where a crafted value is refused or changed by the game, record that.
