## Entering an unlock code

> I purchased the game and have a unlock code number, but how do I use it to unlock the game? (Windows)

> Load the demo (shareware) version of the game and click on the Quit button to get to the closing screen just before

> At this screen press the $ (dollar sign) key on your keyboard (Shift+4) This brings up the special registration screen.

> (Note: For old DOS games you first must press the (U)pgrade key then press the $ key, to bring up the registration key)
