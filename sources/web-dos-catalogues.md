# Catalogue and archive pages for DOS Isle Wars (secondary)

- **Kind**: secondary sources: shareware catalogues, a review site and an archive item page, none of
  them the publisher. **Never `doc`**: they are where the release year, the DOS claim and the
  shareware-versus-registered split are tested, and each cell they touch stays `unknown`.
- **Retrieved**: 2026-10-02, the pages only (viewed as text). **Nothing was downloaded**: the pages'
  download, torrent and emulator links were not followed, and no archive item was fetched.
- **Scope**: DOS *Isle Wars* (all four pages). None mentions *Isle Wars Pro* except where stated.
- **Hazards met**: no instruction was found in any page's text. The pages have advertising and
  redirect links in the body, which are data and were not followed. The classicdosgames.com page
  has an undated sentence about selling that malpaco copied; see "Availability" below.

## classicdosgames.com

<https://www.classicdosgames.com/game/Isle_Wars.html>.
Archived, first capture 2010-01-03: <https://web.archive.org/web/20100103234421/http://www.classicdosgames.com/game/Isle_Wars.html>
(read 2026-10-03; it carries the availability sentence quoted below)

> Isle Wars 1991

> Battle takes place among 46 countries divided between 9 continents of varying size.

> The shareware version pits you against 3 computer opponents, and allows customization of difficulty level and the number of armies at the beginning of the game.

> The registered version allows up to four humans to play.

The page's description is the source malpaco's "46 countries divided between 9 continents" row
matches. The publisher's own DOS page speaks of "islands"
([DOS games page](web-soleau-dos-games.md)) and gives no count.

### Availability

> Soleau Software still sells the registered version of this game for $12 by download.

The live page's footer reads "Copyright 2005-2026" and the sentence itself carries no date. The
archived capture of 2010-01-03 (link above) already holds the same sentence, with the footer
"Copyright 2005-2010", so the sentence dates to 2010-01-03 or earlier: it is at least sixteen years
old and cannot be read as a statement about 2026. The publisher's own DOS page in 2012 gave "$8
apiece" and its 1997 order page gave "$12" for one DOS game
([ordering](web-soleau-ordering.md#1997-online-ordering-page-captured-1997-10-22)). The sentence is
the likely origin of the malpaco row.
